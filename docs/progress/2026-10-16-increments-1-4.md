## Problems found and fixed during verification

- The first accent colour (`#D9541E`) measured 4.0:1 with white text under axe; replaced with `#C84A18` (4.7:1), with a separate `accent-bright` for accent text on the dark background. Design notes updated.
- WCAG 2.2 target-size failures on footer links and breadcrumbs (17 px tall) and on the skip link, whose off-screen 1×1 box overlapped header controls; fixed with padding and a true off-screen position.
- Hidden form inputs lost their values on re-render because a hidden input's `.value` mirrors its attribute; they are now written by the DOM only.
- Google Fonts was unreachable at build time; Inter is now self-hosted, which also removes a third-party dependency from every build and page load.
- React 19's compiler lint rejected setState-in-effect patterns in the navigation; menus now close on link click instead of watching the route.
